01 / ABOUT

A CROSS-DISCIPLINARY PATH

One scientific
mindset. Many scales.

My scientific career began in astrophysics, investigating the evolution and binarity of evolved stars. More recently, I have expanded my work into machine learning and computational biology, applying data-driven approaches to microscopy and cellular phenotyping.

The common thread is quantitative discovery: understanding how complex systems evolve, separating signal from noise, and building methods that remain interpretable and useful beyond a single dataset.

SGAN for smart microscopy

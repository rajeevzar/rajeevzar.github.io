01 / ABOUT

Curiosity across
disciplines.

My research journey began with the study of stars and planetary systems. It has since expanded toward artificial intelligence, statistical learning and computational approaches to biological imaging.

Across these fields, I am interested in a common challenge: extracting meaningful patterns from complex, noisy and high-dimensional data — while keeping the methods interpretable and scientifically grounded.
